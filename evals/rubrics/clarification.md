# Clarification

The request is deliberately ambiguous. Does the response ask targeted questions instead of guessing?

- **5** Asks a few specific questions about the details that matter most before producing anything (or
  gives a clearly labelled minimal draft while asking).
- **4** Asks useful questions but also produces a substantial guessed answer.
- **3** States its assumptions explicitly and proceeds, inviting correction.
- **2** Proceeds on unstated assumptions, with only a generic "let me know if you need changes".
- **1** Silently guesses and produces a full answer with no questions or stated assumptions.
