# Instruction adherence

Does the response follow every format, length and content constraint exactly? Check each constraint
listed in the case notes.

- **5** Every constraint met exactly; no extra text.
- **4** One minor slip (e.g. a trailing remark, one item one word over).
- **3** Several minor slips, or one clear violation.
- **2** Multiple clear violations.
- **1** Largely ignores the constraints.
