# Creativity

Is the output varied and original? Score originality and variety only, not polish or correctness.

- **5** Surprising, non-obvious ideas; items differ meaningfully from each other; no cliches.
- **4** Mostly fresh, with one or two predictable items.
- **3** A mix of competent but familiar ideas.
- **2** Mostly the first ideas anyone would think of; items are similar to each other.
- **1** Entirely generic or cliched output.
